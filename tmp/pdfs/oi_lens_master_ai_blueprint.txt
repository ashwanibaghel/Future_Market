ban sake.

Chapter 14. Guiding Principles
1. Dataset first, models later.
2. Every feature must be explainable.
3. Never overwrite raw market data.
4. Every derived value must be versioned.
5. Every prediction must include historical evidence.
6. Models must remain specialized, not monolithic.
7. Validate everything before training.
8. Trading decisions must always be explainable.
9. Improve dataset quality in every sprint.
10. Long-term consistency is more important than short-term accuracy.
Final Vision
OI Lens is not being built to predict the next candle.
It is being built to become a continuously learning Quantitative Market Intelligence Platform
that understands market structure, recognizes recurring patterns, measures probabilities from
historical evidence, and assists traders with transparent, data-backed execution decisions.
The long-term goal is to create a platform where every recommendation is supported by
measurable evidence, every model has a clear responsibility, and every improvement begins with
higher-quality data. Over time, this architecture can evolve into a world-class research and
decision-support system capable of powering advanced trading intelligence rather than relying on
opaque "buy/sell" predictions.
