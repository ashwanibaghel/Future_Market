## 🧠 THE DEFINITION OF MARKET EXPERIENCE

> **"Experience = Memory + Outcome + Reflection"**

- **Memory**: What happened in the market (`Situation Timeline`).
- **Outcome**: How the market physically resolved across multiple horizons (`5m`, `15m`, `30m`, `60m`, `EOD`, `NEXT_DAY`).
- **Reflection**: Counterfactual analysis and structural generalization (*"Why did this succeed or fail?"*).

---

## 📊 FUTURE CONFIDENCE DUALITY
- **Evidence Confidence**: Empirical strength of observed data (`Evidence -> Confidence`).
- **Outcome Reliability**: Historical win-rate / success frequency of similar structural episodes.

---

## 📚 MEMORY TAXONOMY EVOLUTION (PHASE 2 & BEYOND)

1. **Episodic Memory**: State-based continuous market episodes with multi-horizon outcomes (Sprint AB).
2. **Semantic Memory**: High-level market rules, structural concepts, and regime principles.
3. **Procedural Memory**: Execution tactics, liquidity routing, and spread management strategies.
4. **Failure Memory**: Historically documented breakdown scenarios and false breakout patterns.
5. **Rare Event Memory**: Black swan, flash crash, and extreme volatility expansion events.
