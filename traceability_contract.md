# OI Lens Traceability Contract

> **100% Empirical Traceability Guarantee Across Cognitive AI Layers**

---

## 📜 THE 5-TIER TRACEABILITY CHAIN

Every Decision Support Assessment produced by the AI must be 100% traceable back to its underlying empirical evidence without black-box gaps:

```text
Tier 5: Decision Support Assessment (Sprint AF)
               ↓ [Traces to]
Tier 4: Reasoning Chain & Competing Hypotheses (Sprint AE)
               ↓ [Traces to]
Tier 3: Synthesized Empirical Evidence & Outcomes (Sprint AD)
               ↓ [Traces to]
Tier 2: Immutable Episodic Memory Hash IDs (Sprint AB & AC)
               ↓ [Traces to]
Tier 1: Situation Timelines & MECE Observations (Sprint Z & AA)
               ↓ [Traces to]
Tier 0: Canonical Option Chain Snapshots (Sprint Y Data Lake)
```

---

## 📜 NON-NEGOTIABLE GUARANTEES
1. **Zero Untraceable Outputs**: If an assessment cannot supply its exact 5-tier trace back to Tier 0 snapshot timestamps, it is invalidated.
2. **Auditability**: Quantitative research engineers can inspect the exact Memory IDs and Evidence metrics for any decision support object.
