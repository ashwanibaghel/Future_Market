# OI Lens Cognitive Brain Constitution

> **Operating System Philosophy & Non-Negotiable Governance Rules for OI Lens AI Systems**

---

## 📜 ARTICLE I — THE CORE PERCEPTION & UNDERSTANDING PRINCIPLE

> **"The Perception and Situation Engines shall never directly infer trading decisions, buy/sell signals, or entry/exit predictions. Their sole responsibility is to answer: 'What is happening in the market right now?' NOT 'What should I do?'"**

---

## 📜 ARTICLE II — THE EXPLAINABILITY GUARANTEE

1. **Zero Black-Box Outputs**: No cognitive layer (Perception, Situation, Memory, Reasoning, Decision) shall emit an output without explicit quantitative `evidence` and human-readable `reasoning`.
2. **Explicit Unknowns Declaration**: Every analytical object must explicitly declare `unknowns` — data or confirmations that are inconclusive, missing, or unavailable. A true Market Intelligence System explicitly states what it does NOT know to prevent hallucination.

---

## 📜 ARTICLE III — COGNITIVE LAYER SEPARATION & ARCHITECTURE

The Artificial Trader Brain strictly obeys a 5-layer decoupled cognitive hierarchy:

```text
Layer 1: Perception (Observe)                 --> Sprint Z (Completed)
Layer 2: Situation Understanding (Context)       --> Sprint AA (Completed)
Layer 3: Market Memory Formation (Experience)    --> Sprint AB (Current)
Layer 4: Experience Generalization & Reasoning   --> Sprint AC
Layer 5: Decision Intelligence Engine           --> Sprint AD
```

- **Lower layers shall never pollute upper layers with trade signals.**
- **Upper layers shall inherit full explainable evidence from lower layers.**

---

## 📜 ARTICLE IV — TIME CONTINUITY & STATE EVOLUTION

1. **Markets are non-static**: Situations and episodes must be tracked as state-driven evolving timelines ($t_0 \dots t_k$) rather than isolated static snapshots or arbitrary fixed clock ticks.
2. **Evolution Phases**: All situations must track explicit state evolution:
   `BUILDING` $\rightarrow$ `SUSTAINED` $\rightarrow$ `ACCELERATING` $\rightarrow$ `WEAKENING` $\rightarrow$ `DISSIPATING`.

---

## 📜 ARTICLE V — MEMORY BEFORE REASONING

Before reasoning or trade decision making, the AI must form persistent **Episodic Market Memories**. Reasoning without memory is scratch calculation; reasoning WITH memory is **Accumulated Market Experience**.

---

## 📜 ARTICLE VI — OUTCOME BEFORE LEARNING

> **"Decision $\rightarrow$ Outcome $\rightarrow$ Learning"**

No cognitive learning model or memory index can evaluate effectiveness without grounding in actual physical market outcomes. Every memory episode must encode post-episode outcome metrics across multiple time horizons (`5m`, `15m`, `30m`, `60m`, `EOD`, `NEXT_DAY`).

---

## 📜 ARTICLE VII — KNOWLEDGE ENGINE PHILOSOPHY

The AI does not rely on fragile, arbitrary IF-THEN rule constructs. It employs a **Knowledge Engine** that maps market structural dynamics (Open Interest, Volume, Price Structure, Expiry Pinning) into explainable structural interpretations.

---

## 📜 ARTICLE VIII — EVIDENCE BEFORE CONFIDENCE

> **"Evidence $\rightarrow$ Confidence (NEVER Confidence $\rightarrow$ Evidence)"**

Confidence scores shall never exist independently or be arbitrarily hardcoded. Confidence must always be mathematically derived from an explicit, quantitative `evidence` pool. This prevents AI hallucination and ensures 100% empirical grounding.

---

## 📜 ARTICLE IX — MEMORY IMMUTABILITY

> **"Once a Market Memory Episode is created, its observed facts shall never be modified or rewritten. Corrections, reinterpretations, or reflections must create new metadata objects, never rewrite history."**

Every historical memory episode is locked forever to ensure 100% data integrity and prevent historical revisionism in AI training datasets.
